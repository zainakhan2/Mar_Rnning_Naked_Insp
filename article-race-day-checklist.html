Race Day Checklist: Everything You Need (Print This)

Race morning is not the time to think. Race morning is the time to execute a checklist you've already prepared. Print this. Tape it to your mirror. Check every item before you leave the house.

The Night Before

  • Pin your bib — or attach with clips. Do this now, not at 5 AM with shaky hands
  • Charge your phone + watch — 100% before bed
  • Lay out everything — shoes, socks, shorts/tights, sports bra, shirt, running belt, gels, body glide, hair ties. Everything visible, nothing to search for
  • Pack your post-race bag — dry clothes, flip-flops, snacks, phone charger, cash for parking
  • Set 2 alarms — your phone AND a backup. Race morning oversleeping is a real nightmare
  • Eat dinner by 7 PM — something you've eaten before. No new foods within 48 hours of race
  • Sleep — you won't sleep well. That's normal. The sleep 2 nights before matters more

Race Morning (3 Hours Before Start)

  • Breakfast — whatever you've practiced in training. Toast + banana + coffee is classic. Eat at least 2 hours before gun time
  • Body Glide — inner thighs, sports bra band, underarms, nipples (yes, women too), anywhere your running belt contacts skin
  • Sunscreen — even if cloudy. You're outside for 2-5 hours
  • Bathroom — at home, before leaving. The port-a-potty line at the start is 30+ minutes

Load Your Belt

If you're using a running belt (and for half/full marathon, you should be):

  • Phone — charged, airplane mode OFF (emergency), Strava/Nike Run Club loaded, music downloaded offline
  • Gels — 1 per 45 minutes of expected running time. Half marathon: 3-4 gels. Full: 6-8 gels. Practice opening them with one hand
  • Fuel timing card — optional but helpful: a small card that says "Gel 1: mile 6, Gel 2: mile 9" etc.
  • ID + cash/card — in case of emergency. $20 bill = backup for post-race food/Uber
  • Period products — if applicable. Hidden pocket = no stress

At the Start Line

  • Arrive 45-60 minutes early — parking, bib pickup (if not done yesterday), bathroom, warmup
  • Throwaway layer — old sweatshirt from Goodwill ($3). Wear it to stay warm, discard at the start. Races donate discarded clothing
  • Start at the BACK — the back 30%. You will pass people throughout the race, which feels amazing. Starting at the front and being passed feels terrible
  • Run YOUR pace — the crowd starts fast. The adrenaline starts fast. You should not start fast. First mile should feel easy. If it doesn't: slow down

After the Finish

  • Keep walking — do not sit down immediately. Walk for 5-10 minutes to prevent blood pooling
  • Eat within 30 minutes — protein + carbs. The race-provided banana + bagel works. Your body needs fuel to start repair
  • Stretch gently — not aggressively. Light stretching, foam rolling if available
  • Photo — you earned it. Get the medal photo while you still have the post-race glow
  • Text your people — they've been tracking you. Let them know you're alive and smiling
