Heading Font

Weights: 600 (semi), 700 (bold), 800 (extra-bold). Used for: all headings, stat callouts, navigation, buttons.

Body Font

Weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium). Used for: body text, descriptions, captions, form labels. Line height: 1.7.

The wordmark uses Plus Jakarta Sans Extra Bold. "Stride" in the primary color, "Her" in Desert Rose. Minimum clear space: height of the "H" on all sides. Never stretch, rotate, or add effects.

Brand Voice

We Are

Warm, confident, knowledgeable. We talk to runners like a friend who happens to know a lot about running gear. Direct but never aggressive. Empowering but never preachy. We acknowledge periods, body diversity, and different paces without making it a big deal — because it shouldn't be.

We Are Not

Corporate, clinical, or condescending. We don't use "female jogger." We don't say "for ladies." We don't use pink-washed marketing. We don't gatekeep running pace, distance, or experience. We never make assumptions about what women can or can't do.
