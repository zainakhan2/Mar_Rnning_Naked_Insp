Executive Summary

The complete strategic overview of StrideHer — product, market, financials, and execution plan in one narrative.

The Opportunity

35 million women run regularly in the United States. They use running belts designed as "unisex" — meaning designed for straight-hipped male torsos and marketed to everyone. The result: belts that ride up on women's hips, single-pocket designs where phone and keys tangle together, and zero consideration for women-specific carry needs.

StrideHer is the first running belt designed specifically for women's hip geometry. Contoured 4-panel profile, 3 separate zippered pockets (phone + fuel + valuables with hidden period pocket), 200+ silicone grip dots for zero bounce, fully adjustable 25"-43" waist. Retail price: $34.99 with free US shipping.

Market Validation

12,400 monthly Google searches for "women's running belt." No incumbent brand claims this positioning — FlipBelt, SPIbelt, and Nathan all market as unisex. The TAM is $2.1B (US running gear market), SAM is $180M (running belts), and StrideHer's SOM target is $500K-$2M (0.3-1.1% of SAM) within 3 years.

Women's running participation has grown 32% since 2019. The parkrun movement (2M+ registered UK runners) creates a grassroots channel no predecessor has exploited for women's running gear specifically.

Go-To-Market Strategy

Channel strategy: Shopify D2C (primary, Month 1) → Amazon (Month 3-4) → wholesale to specialty running stores (Month 6-9) → race expos (Month 9-12) → TikTok Shop + Instagram Shopping (Month 5-6) → International Amazon UK (Month 9-12).

Marketing: organic-first with paid amplification. Pre-launch email waitlist (200+ subscribers) → launch day email + social blitz → Meta Ads ($10-15/day) → Google PMax ($13/day, Week 2) → influencer seeding (micro/nano, 5-10 per wave) → parkrun grassroots → content marketing (6 SEO blog articles) → review-driven social proof flywheel.

Manufacturing: Alibaba supplier, 600-unit first run, YKK zippers mandatory, AQL 2.5/4.0 quality standard, ocean freight 55-65 days, safety stock trigger at 338 units.

Competitive Moat

A competitor can copy the product features in 3 months. They cannot copy: 500+ verified reviews, 5,000+ social followers, 50+ ambassador relationships, SEO authority for "women's running belt," and 18 months of brand equity with the women's running community. The moat is the brand, not the belt.
