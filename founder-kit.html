🚀 Founder Kit

Read this first. 10 steps from "I have an idea" to "orders are shipping." Everything else in this GTM package supports these 10 steps.

01

Form the Business

Wyoming LLC ($100-300) + EIN + Mercury bank account + Wave accounting. Takes 1-2 weeks. Everything else depends on this.

📄 P7A, P8A → Business Setup
02

Secure the Product

Send RFQ to 3 suppliers (use P20 bilingual templates). Approve sample. Place 600-unit PO with 30% deposit. 35 days production + 30-35 days freight.

📄 P3A, P20 → Production Planning
03

Build the Store

Shopify Basic + Dawn theme. Apply brand colors (Rose/Sage/Ocean/Navy). Build product page. Install Klaviyo, GA4, Meta Pixel, Judge.me. 4 legal pages. 27-point QA.

📄 P4A, Theme Config → Theme Setup
04

Create Content

Batch-produce 20 pieces in one 5-hour session. Use the 30-day calendar (P23) and video scripts (P24). Film on phone, natural light, one take.

📄 P23, P24 → Calendar · Scripts
05

Build the Waitlist

Launch email signup. Target 200+ before launch. Post 4-5×/week on IG + TikTok. Ship 5-10 influencer packages. Build anticipation.

📄 P1E, P5A → Pre-Launch Community
06

Set Up Fulfillment

Build packing station ($50-250). Write SOP (10 steps, 90 sec/order). Stock shelving by SKU. Process 3 test orders. Enroll in USPS daily pickup.

📄 P10A → Home Fulfillment
07

Launch

Remove Shopify password. Send launch email. Activate Meta Ads ($10-15/day). Post launch content. Fulfill same-day. Respond to every email <4 hours. Write handwritten notes.

📄 P6B → Launch Week
08

Optimize Weekly

15-metric KPI review every Sunday. A/B test one thing per week. Activate Google Ads Week 2. Respond to every review. Weekly inventory count.

📄 P15, P6E → Task Tracker
09

Scale Monthly

Monthly financial review (5 reports). Launch Amazon (Month 3-4). Wholesale outreach. 2-belt bundle. Race expos. Hire VA when 10+ orders/day.

📄 P11A-CD → Projections
10

Build the Brand

Year 1: prove product-market fit. Year 2: product #2 + international. Year 3: the brand synonymous with "women's running belt." The moat is the brand, not the belt.
