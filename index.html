🏔️ Brand Manifesto & Business Rationale
For internal stakeholders, investors & team — why this brand exists and how we win.

The Thesis

35 million women run regularly in the US. Every running belt they use was designed for men. The fueling science changed. The shoe technology changed. But running belts haven't changed — they're flat elastic bands designed around straight-hipped male torsos and marketed as "unisex." We built the first belt designed around women's hip geometry, pocket needs, and carry requirements — including a period pocket that 0 of 42 competitors offer.

🎯 Category Gap

$34.99 women-specific running belt in a market where every competitor markets as "unisex." 12,400 monthly Google searches. Zero incumbent claims this positioning.

🔧 Engineering

Contoured 4-panel profile, 200+ silicone grip dots (hexagonal pattern), 3 separate YKK-zippered pockets, magnetic snap fuel pocket, Duraflex adjustable buckle.

📦 Channel Strategy

Shopify DTC (Month 1, 50% margin) → Amazon FBA (Month 3-4, 35% margin) → Wholesale (Month 6-9) → TikTok Shop + Race Expos → Amazon UK (Month 9-12).

📈 Growth Path

Year 1: prove PMF ($55-75K). Year 2: product #2 + UK expansion ($200-400K). Year 3: the brand synonymous with women's running gear ($400-700K).
