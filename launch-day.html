🚀 Launch Day — Hour by Hour

Structured D0 plan from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Every action timed and sequenced. Source: P6B Launch Week Execution.

6:00 AM
☕ Final prep

Review this checklist. Coffee. Deep breath. Today changes everything.

6:30 AM
🔓 REMOVE SHOPIFY PASSWORD

Online Store → Preferences → uncheck password. Your store is LIVE. Verify homepage loads on phone + desktop.

6:35 AM
📧 SEND LAUNCH EMAIL

Klaviyo: trigger the launch email to your full waitlist. Subject: "We're Live 🏃‍♀️ | HERSTRIDE20 for 20% off." Verify delivery + click tracking.

6:45 AM
📱 POST LAUNCH CONTENT

Instagram: launch Reel + 3 Stories. TikTok: launch video. Caption: "WE'RE LIVE!" + product details + HERSTRIDE20 code. Pin to profile.

7:00 AM
📣 ACTIVATE META ADS

Ads Manager → turn on all 3 ad sets (interest, LAL, broad). Budget: $15/day. Verify ads are approved and spending.

7:15 AM
🔍 Verify everything works

Place a test order yourself. Check: payment processing ✓ order confirmation email ✓ Klaviyo triggers ✓ GA4 tracking ✓ Meta Pixel fires ✓.

8:00 AM
📊 First metrics check

GA4: real-time users. Shopify: orders dashboard. Klaviyo: email open rate. Meta: ad impressions + spend. Note everything in a spreadsheet.

9:00 AM
📱 Stories wave 2

Post 3 more Stories: behind-the-scenes, packing station ready, "your orders are about to ship" excitement. Reply to every DM and comment.

10:00 AM
📧 Check support inbox

Respond to every email within 2 hours today. Common Day 1 questions: sizing, shipping time, discount code issues. Use P9A templates.

12:00 PM
📊 Midday metrics review

Check: total orders, revenue, ad spend, ROAS, cart abandonment rate. If 0 orders by noon: don't panic — check traffic first. If traffic >50 but 0 orders: review pricing/product page.

12:30 PM
📱 Midday content push

New Reel or TikTok: "3 hours in and we've shipped [X] orders!" or product demo if not yet posted. Engage with every comment.

2:00 PM
📦 FULFILLMENT BATCH #1

Process ALL orders received so far. Pick → pack (poly mailer + insert card + handwritten note) → print Shopify labels → seal → USPS pickup/dropoff. Target: 90 seconds per order.

3:00 PM
✍️ Handwritten notes

Write personal thank-you notes for today's orders. "Thank you for being one of our very first customers. You're part of something special." First 20 orders get handwritten notes.

4:00 PM
📊 Afternoon metrics

Update the Day 1 tracker. Orders, revenue, ad ROAS, email open rate, social engagement. Take screenshots of Shopify dashboard for your records.

5:00 PM
📱 Stories wave 3

Evening Stories: "Day 1 update — [X] orders shipped! Thank you 🥹" Show the packing station, sealed mailers, USPS receipt.

6:00 PM
📧 Respond to ALL remaining emails

Clear inbox to zero. Every customer who emailed today gets a response before you sleep. Speed = trust on Day 1.

7:00 PM
📦 FULFILLMENT BATCH #2

Process any orders from afternoon. Same protocol. Label, pack, seal. These ship tomorrow morning.

8:00 PM
📊 End of Day 1 Review

Record final numbers: total orders, total revenue, total ad spend, ROAS, email open rate, social followers gained, support emails received. Compare to projections.

8:30 PM
📧 Send Day 1 thank-you to waitlist

Quick email: "Day 1 is done — [X] orders from [X] states. Thank you for believing in this." Builds community feeling.

9:00 PM
🎯 Day 2 prep

Review Meta Ads: any ad set underperforming? Adjust. Prep tomorrow's content. Refill packing supplies if needed. Set 2 alarms.

10:00 PM
😴 Sleep

You did it. Day 1 is done. Tomorrow you do it all again — but now you're a founder with customers. Rest. You earned it.
