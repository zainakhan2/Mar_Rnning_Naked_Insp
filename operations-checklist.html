🔄 Recurring Operations Checklist

Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual cadence. Based on P22 Annual Operations Calendar. Print this and tape it to your wall.

📅 DAILY (Every Business Day)
  • □Check Shopify dashboard: new orders, revenue, conversion rate
  • □Fulfill all orders by 2 PM (same-day shipping commitment)
  • □Respond to every customer email/DM within 4 hours
  • □Check Meta + Google ad dashboards: spend, ROAS, CPC
  • □Post 1 Instagram Story (BTS, poll, product, or repost)
  • □Monitor reviews: respond to any new review within 48 hours
  • □Check inventory levels: any SKU below 20 units = alert
📊 WEEKLY (Every Sunday Evening)
  • □KPI review: 15 metrics (revenue, orders, AOV, conversion, traffic, email signups, social followers, review count, ad spend, ROAS, CAC, return rate, inventory levels, cash position, customer emails)
  • □Inventory reconciliation: physical count vs Shopify count
  • □Amazon Search Term Report: download, identify new converting keywords → add to Campaign 1 exact match
  • □Meta Ads: kill any ad set with <1.5× ROAS after $20 spend. Scale any with >3× ROAS by 20%
  • □Google Ads: review PMax performance, adjust budget if ROAS target met
  • □Batch-schedule next week's social content (4-5 feed posts)
  • □Review customer support trends: any recurring issue = product/process improvement
  • □Update cash flow tracker (P15) with actual revenue and expenses
📈 MONTHLY (1st of Each Month)
  • □Full financial review: P&L, cash position, budget vs actual (5 reports)
  • □Monthly return summary: top 3 reasons, any product improvement actions
  • □Inventory planning: project stockout date, trigger reorder if needed
  • □Email list health: growth rate, open rate, unsubscribe rate
  • □Social media audit: follower growth, top 3 performing posts, content pillar mix
  • □Competitor check: any new entrants? Price changes? New features?
  • □Update Scenario Tracker (P16): which scenario trajectory are we on?
  • □Owner's Draw decision: can the business sustain a draw this month? (G4 framework)
  • □Site speed check: PageSpeed Insights score (target: all green)
  • □Blog: publish 1 new article (draw from the 15-article library)
🏆 QUARTERLY (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct)
  • □Customer survey: NPS + 6 questions via Klaviyo (P9B)
  • □Product roadmap review: is v2 justified by customer feedback?
  • □Channel performance assessment: apply triple-fail test (G8) to each channel
  • □Wholesale outreach wave: email 5 new specialty running stores
  • □Influencer wave: identify + seed 5-10 new micro-influencers
  • □Content audit: which blog articles drive traffic? Refresh top 3
  • □Financial forecast update: adjust Year 1 projections based on actuals
  • □Supplier relationship check: any quality trends? Negotiate better terms if volume justifies
📋 ANNUAL (December)
  • □Full year financial close: annual P&L, total revenue, total profit, effective tax rate
  • □Write "Lessons Learned" document: top 5 things that worked, top 5 that didn't
  • □Year 2 plan: product expansion, channel expansion, team scaling
  • □Brand health assessment: reviews sentiment, social growth, media mentions
  • □Supplier annual review: quality scorecard, pricing renegotiation, v2 modifications
  • □Charitable giving audit: total donated, impact report for community
  • □Update 3-year vision document with Year 1 actuals vs projections
  • □Tax preparation: compile all records for CPA
