📅 Post-Launch 90-Day Plan

Week-by-week optimization plan from Day 1 through Day 90. PPC optimization, keyword harvesting, content publishing, review velocity, and channel expansion.

PHASE 1: STABILIZE (Days 1-14)

Week 1 (Days 1-7)

  • □Fulfill every order same-day. Response time <4 hours.
  • □Meta Ads: monitor daily. Kill ad sets with ROAS <1.5× after $20. Scale winners by 20%.
  • □Track: daily orders, revenue, ad spend, ROAS, conversion rate, cart abandonment.
  • □Post daily on IG + TikTok (launch week = maximum content frequency).
  • □Send post-purchase email to every buyer (if not automated, do manually).
  • □Collect first UGC: DM customers, ask for photos/reviews.

Week 2 (Days 8-14)

  • □First weekly KPI review: 15 metrics in P15 tracker.
  • □Activate Google PMax at $13/day.
  • □Respond to first reviews within 48 hours (use star-specific templates from P9B).
  • □First inventory reconciliation: physical count vs Shopify.
  • □Run first A/B test: product page title OR hero image.
  • □Download Amazon PPC Search Term Report: harvest converting keywords.
  • □Settle into 4-5 posts/week content rhythm.
PHASE 2: OPTIMIZE (Days 15-45)

Week 3-4 (Days 15-28)

  • □Second influencer wave: ship 5-10 more packages based on Wave 1 performance.
  • □Attend 1 local parkrun wearing StrideHer. Hand out 10 cards.
  • □Meta Ads: first creative refresh. Swap lowest-performing ad for new variant.
  • □Begin Amazon PPC keyword migration: move converting broad terms → exact match in Campaign 1.
  • □Add negative keywords to campaigns based on Search Term Report waste.
  • □Publish blog article #1 to Shopify (from 15-article library).
  • □Review customer support trends: any recurring sizing/quality issue?

Week 5-6 (Days 29-45)

  • □Monthly financial review: P&L, cash position, budget vs actual.
  • □Monthly return summary: top 3 reasons → product improvement pipeline.
  • □Launch Amazon listing: FBA enrollment, Vine program, Sponsored Products.
  • □Set up TikTok Shop + Instagram Shopping.
  • □Create VIP segment in Klaviyo (2+ orders or $60+ lifetime).
  • □Launch 2-belt bundle ($59.99, save $10).
  • □Day 45: activate cross-sell email flow.
  • □Publish blog article #2.
  • □Target: 20+ reviews across Shopify + Amazon.
PHASE 3: SCALE (Days 46-90)

Week 7-8 (Days 46-60)

  • □Second monthly financial review.
  • □Batch-create 20 more content pieces (second 30-day content cycle).
  • □Create wholesale one-sheet + email 5 specialty running stores.
  • □First quarterly customer survey: NPS + 6 questions.
  • □Amazon PPC: campaign maturity check. Target ACoS <30%.
  • □Publish blog article #3.
  • □Enhance size guide with real customer fit photos.

Week 9-12 (Days 61-90)

  • □Month 3 breakeven trajectory checkpoint (are we on track for Month 5-6 breakeven?).
  • □Apply to 2-3 race expos (Q3-Q4 events from expo calendar).
  • □Mine reviews for ad copy: extract best phrases for new Meta/Google creatives.
  • □Build running media relationships: pitch Women's Running, Runner's World.
  • □Begin operations manual: compile all SOPs into Google Drive.
  • □2-week time audit: track founder hours → identify first hire need.
  • □Evaluate 3PL: needed if 10+ orders/day for 30 consecutive days.
  • □Publish blog articles #4 and #5.
  • □Target: 50+ total reviews, $4,000+ monthly revenue, clear breakeven trajectory.
