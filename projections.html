Scenario-Specific Action Plans
Your ACTUAL Month 1 revenue tells you which scenario you're in. Follow the corresponding action plan.

🔴 Pessimistic Actions

If Month 1 revenue < $800

  • Month 2: Cut ads to $5/day. Focus 100% organic. Activate Launch Rescue (G3).
  • Month 3: Diagnose PMF. Survey non-buyers. Test $29.99 price for 2 weeks.
  • Month 4: If cash < $3K: preservation mode. $0 ads. $200/month burn only.
  • Month 6: Pivot assessment: reposition, reprice, add product, or push wholesale.
  • Month 9: If still unprofitable: exit assessment (liquidate, pivot, or continue lean).

🟡 Moderate Actions

If Month 1 revenue $800-$2,000

  • Month 1-2: On track. Execute P13. Focus: fulfillment quality, review collection, ad optimization.
  • Month 3: Scale ads to $15/day Meta. Launch Google PMax. Begin Amazon listing.
  • Month 5-6: Breakeven! Start Owner's Draw (10-20% net profit). Activate VIP segment.
  • Month 9: Hire VA ($360/month). Apply to 3 expos. Wholesale outreach to 5 stores.
  • Month 12: Year 1 complete. Impact report. Lessons doc. Plan Year 2 expansion.

🟢 Optimistic Actions

If Month 1 revenue > $2,000

  • Month 1: Confirm fulfillment handles 10+/day. Calculate stockout date. Consider early reorder.
  • Month 2-3: Scale ads $20/day. Launch Amazon early. Begin wholesale outreach.
  • Month 3-4: Early reorder: 800-1000 units. Air freight bridge if needed ($300-500).
  • Month 5-6: Hire VA immediately. Evaluate 3PL. Launch Amazon UK. First expo.
  • Month 9-12: Year 2 targets hit early. $80-120K annual. 2nd hire. Product #2 validation.
