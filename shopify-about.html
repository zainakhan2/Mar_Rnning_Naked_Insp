About StrideHer

StrideHer started with a question: why are all running belts designed the same way?

I'm [YOUR NAME], a runner and the founder of StrideHer. After trying four different running belts — all marketed as "unisex" — I realized the problem wasn't my body. It was the belts. They were designed around a straight-hipped male torso and marketed to everyone.

So I built a belt designed around women's bodies. Contoured panels that follow the hip curve. Three separate pockets because your phone shouldn't share space with your sticky gel wrapper. A hidden pocket for period products because apparently nobody else thought women might need that. And 200+ silicone grip dots because bounce gets worse with every mile.

Our Purpose

Making running accessible and empowering for ALL women.

Every pace. Every body. Every mile. We believe "runner" means anyone who runs — regardless of speed, distance, or experience. We sponsor women's race entries every month because running should never be limited by finances.

Our Promise

30-day money-back guarantee. Free size exchanges. If it doesn't meet your expectations, we'll make it right — no questions asked. We're a small brand with a big commitment to every single customer.
