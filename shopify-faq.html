Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to expand the answer.

Q: What size should I order?
Measure your waist where you'd wear the belt (about 2 inches below belly button). S/M fits 25"-33". L/XL fits 33"-43". If you're right at 33": go L/XL — it adjusts down, and we offer free exchanges.
Q: Will it fit my phone?
Yes — fits up to iPhone 16 Pro Max with a case. The phone has its own separate zippered pocket.
Q: Does it bounce?
No. 200+ silicone grip dots lock the belt in place. Our testers run 13+ miles without adjusting once.
Q: Is it machine washable?
Hand wash cold recommended. Gentle cycle in a mesh bag works too — just skip the dryer.
Q: What's in the hidden pocket?
The valuables pocket has a hidden interior mesh section designed for period products — because nobody else thought to include that.
Q: What if it doesn't fit?
30-day money-back guarantee + free size exchanges. We'll ship the right size today. Zero risk.
Q: Do you ship internationally?
Yes! UK ($14.99), Canada ($12.99), EU ($16.99), Australia ($16.99). Free US shipping on all orders.
Q: How long does shipping take?
US: 2-5 business days. International: 10-21 business days depending on destination.
