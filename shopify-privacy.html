What We Collect

When you make a purchase: name, email, shipping address, payment information (processed securely by Shopify Payments — we never see your full card number). When you browse: cookies for analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising (Meta Pixel).

How We Use It

To fulfill your order, send shipping updates, and improve your shopping experience. If you opt in to email: to send product updates and promotions via Klaviyo. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use: Shopify (e-commerce platform), Klaviyo (email marketing), Google Analytics (website analytics), Meta Pixel (advertising). Each has their own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing hello@strideher.com. EU residents have additional rights under GDPR including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Cookies

We use cookies for: website functionality (required), analytics (Google Analytics), and advertising (Meta Pixel). You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.
