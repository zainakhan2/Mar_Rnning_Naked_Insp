Returns & Refund Policy

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you're not completely satisfied with your StrideHer belt, return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. No questions asked.

Free Size Exchanges

Wrong size? We'll ship the correct size today — free of charge. You don't need to return the original first. We trust you.

Defective Product

If your belt has a manufacturing defect: we'll send a free replacement immediately. No need to return the defective one.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

Email hello@strideher.com with your order number and we'll process it within 24 hours. For exchanges, let us know the new size/color and we'll ship it the same day.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of our receiving your return. The refund will appear on your original payment method within 5-10 business days.
