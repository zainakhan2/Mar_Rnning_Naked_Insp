📏 Find Your Perfect Size

Two sizes. Fully adjustable. Free exchanges if it's not perfect.

How to Measure

  1. Stand naturally — don't suck in
  2. Place two fingers below your belly button — that's where the belt sits
  3. Wrap a tape measure snug (not tight) around that point
  4. Read the number — that's your running belt size
SizeWaist RangeUS Women's Size
S/M25" - 33"Sizes 0-10
L/XL33" - 43"Sizes 10-20+

Between Sizes?

If you measure exactly 33": go with L/XL. It adjusts down easily, and a little extra room is always more comfortable than too tight. Plus, we offer free exchanges — zero risk.

The Jump Test

Put on the belt with your phone and keys loaded. Do 10 jumping jacks. Zero movement = correct fit. Any bouncing = tighten the buckle or switch sizes.
