Filming Rules (Quick Reference)

✅ Do

  • Face a window (natural light)
  • Film VERTICAL (9:16) always
  • Hook in first 1 second
  • Add captions on screen
  • Use trending audio on TikTok
  • Batch-film all 5 in one session
  • Film with AirPods as mic
  • Clean, uncluttered background

❌ Don't

  • Film horizontal for social
  • Start with "hey guys"
  • Use ring light (harsh shadows)
  • Over-edit (trim + captions only)
  • Add transitions or zoom effects
  • Aim for perfection (2 takes max)
  • Film under fluorescent lights
  • Post without captions
